06 — LIMITS

What this is not, yet

This is not predictive maintenance

We can see a machine behaving unlike itself and raise a flag. A flag is a hypothesis, not a forecast. A few weeks of floor data is not a few years, and promising a two-week warning today is the fastest way to lose the room the first time an oven stops without one.

The digitised paper sheets are not in these figures

Five real production sheets were transcribed, but sheets record quantities, not clock times — theirs were reconstructed. Excellent for volumes, useless for timing, so they are kept out of this log rather than quietly averaged in.

Not every batch on the floor is in the system yet

The log covers what the board covers. Coverage grows week by week, and until it is complete these medians describe the traced part of the bakery, not all of it.
